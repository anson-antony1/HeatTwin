## Status (Oct 3, built on `ws2-physio` by A)
- `engine/calibrate.py`: stochastic ensemble Kalman update of `met_scale` from 60-s mean HR every 60 s. The observation model is the inverse of %HRR ≈ %VO₂R (Swain & Leutholtz 1997), using the athlete's drill MET, VO₂max default and sustained ceiling.
- **Cardiovascular drift isn't modelled** (`constants.hr_model.drift_bpm_per_c_core` = 0, status TODO). So `thermo_scale` isn't updated, and late-session HR reads as higher met_scale, the conservative direction. Both facts are in the outputs.
- **Gates:**
  - (a) p95 crossing;
  - (b) at least 3 consecutive minutes over;
  - (c) at least 50 % HR coverage and at least 2 updates.

  Output is "re-forecast shows crossing", "no crossing in re-forecast" or "not enough data", plus `held_by`. Readings outside 30–230 bpm are dropped. All gate values live in `constants.calibration` (DESIGN).
- **Personal baseline:** median/MAD of resting and at-load HR (`personal_baseline`).
- **`POST /hr`, `POST /live/start`:** in `engine/api.py`. The re-forecast re-runs the plan with the updated calibration from plan start; core state isn't assimilated.
- **Replay:** `python -m engine.calibrate fixtures/hr_a07_synthetic.csv --realtime` runs at 10× speed. `fixtures/hr_a07_synthetic.csv` is **synthetic**: generated by our own model with met_scale 1.25. Every output says replay.
- **`engine/ect_optional.py`:** Buller 2013 filter, off unless `research_mode=True`, labelled "research mode — method appears patented". Coefficients are still TODO.
- **Tests:** `engine/tests/test_calibrate.py`. The filter converges to 1.24 ± 0.03 for a true 1.25, a single spike and an impossible value don't move it, and the gates hold without data or persistence.
